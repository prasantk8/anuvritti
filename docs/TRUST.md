# The Trust Contract

*Written for a parent, not a lawyer.*

Anuvritti is built to hold the fleeting moments of your child's early years for fifteen, twenty, or fifty years. Because memories are sacred, our architecture is bound by strict ethical and cryptographic promises.

---

## 1. What We Store and Where
- **Sparks and Notes**: Plaintext notes, audio recordings of your voice and your child's voice, and selected photos.
- **Constellations & Returns**: Deterministic timelines and developmental clusters generated to bring forgotten memories back at the right developmental age.
- **Storage Isolation**: Every family lives in a cryptographically isolated vault. One family cannot touch or see another family's data.

---

## 2. Who Can Read Your Archive
- **You and your family**: Only authenticated members whom you explicitly invite (co-parents, grandparents) can read your vault.
- **Not our support team**: Customer support tools are family-blind. We cannot read your child's memories, listen to your audio files, or view transcripts to help you troubleshoot.
- **Not advertisers or AI data brokers**: Your recordings and notes are never used to train third-party machine learning models or sold to data brokers.

---

## 3. What Happens If We Disappear
- **You own the raw files**: You can export your entire family archive in one click at any time. The export contains standard open formats: `.json` for metadata, `.wav` / `.m4a` for voice, and `.mp4` for annual films.
- **Offline HTML Reader**: Every export bundles a zero-dependency, self-contained offline HTML reader. In twenty years, you can double-click `index.html` on any computer and view your family's archive without any internet connection.
- **Memories Never Held Hostage**: If you stop paying or close your account, you never lose the ability to view, search, download, or export what was already saved.

---

## 4. No Manipulation, No Streaks, No Ads
- We do not have streaks, daily active user targets, badges, or engagement tricks.
- We do not nag you to come back or make you feel guilty for being busy with your actual child.
