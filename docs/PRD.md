# ANUVRITTI
## Founder Vision, Product Constitution & Product Requirements Document
### Version 1.0 — Family Presence, Intent & Memory Platform

> **For the little things you don't want life to erase.**

---

## 0. Why This Document Exists

Anuvritti began as an idea for a parenting app.

It quickly became something more personal.

A father sees a toy, a joke, a healthy recipe, a parenting idea, a movie, a book, a place to visit, a science experiment, a song, a life lesson, a funny reel, or something beautiful and thinks:

> “I want to remember this for my child.”

He saves it.

Then life happens.

The reel disappears into Instagram Saves.

The toy remains in a wishlist.

The recipe gets buried in screenshots.

The movie sits in a YouTube playlist.

The note remains somewhere in WhatsApp.

The voice memory never gets recorded.

The intention is real.

The retrieval is broken.

And childhood keeps moving.

Anuvritti exists to solve that.

Not by helping families consume more.

But by helping them **notice what matters, preserve why it mattered, bring it back at the right moment, and turn it into something actually lived together.**

The initial product is deliberately designed for one father and one son.

If it becomes meaningful for one real family, it may later become useful to many.

# 1. The Core Product Thesis

## The Problem

Modern digital life is extraordinarily good at discovery.

It is terrible at meaning.

We constantly encounter things that matter:

- things we want to buy for our children later;
- activities we want to try together;
- foods we want to cook;
- jokes we want to tell;
- movies we want to watch;
- lessons we want to teach;
- places we want to visit;
- healthy habits we want to build;
- stories we want our children to hear;
- things that remind us of our own childhood;
- thoughts we want to pass on someday;
- ordinary moments we never want to forget.

Today we scatter these intentions across:

- Instagram Saves;
- YouTube playlists;
- browser bookmarks;
- screenshots;
- Notes;
- WhatsApp-to-self;
- shopping wishlists;
- photo albums;
- cloud drives;
- voice notes;
- memory.

The information often survives.

The **reason it mattered** usually does not.

And almost nothing intelligently returns when it becomes relevant.

---

## The Opportunity

The internet remembers what we clicked.

Anuvritti should remember:

- **why we cared;**
- **who we cared about;**
- **when it may matter;**
- **what we hoped to do;**
- **whether we ever did it;**
- **what happened when we finally did.**

This creates a new lifecycle:

**Discover → Feel → Capture → Understand → Remember → Return → Live → Reflect → Preserve → Rediscover**

---

# 2. The Product in One Sentence

> **Anuvritti is a private family presence system that helps parents capture meaningful intentions and everyday moments, remember why they mattered, and bring them back when they can become real shared experiences.**

A more emotional expression:

> **You noticed something because you love them.  
> We'll help you not lose it.**

---

# 3. What Anuvritti Is Not

Anuvritti is not:

- an AI parent;
- a child surveillance system;
- a parenting performance dashboard;
- an infinite content feed;
- another bookmarking app;
- a toy marketplace;
- a medical diagnosis tool;
- a milestone comparison engine;
- a family social network;
- a chatbot replacing a parent;
- a system designed to maximize screen time.

It may later include selected elements of learning, health, recommendations, memories, activities, family rituals, and commerce.

But these remain secondary to the central purpose:

> **Presence.**

---

# 4. The Emotional Origin

The initial founder question is not:

> “What do parents want?”

It is:

> **“What would I build if this were only ever used by my son and me?”**

The answer:

I want technology to help me:

- be present when physically absent;
- be present when physically nearby but mentally distracted;
- remember what I wanted to do with him;
- preserve things that made me think of him;
- teach him things when the time is right;
- discover experiences together;
- preserve our ordinary and funny moments;
- notice his changing personality;
- preserve my real voice, not just polished advice;
- capture what he is obsessed with right now;
- involve his mother and grandparents;
- pass forward family stories;
- leave pieces of myself for his future;
- reduce digital clutter;
- spend less time organizing parenting;
- spend more time being his father.

---

# 5. The Deeper Problem: Presence

The initial emotional pain may begin with physical distance.

But Anuvritti must serve a broader truth:

> **A parent can be physically present and emotionally absent.**

Work, phones, meetings, stress, travel, responsibilities, scrolling, fatigue, and fragmented attention quietly steal presence.

Therefore Anuvritti is not merely about remote parenting.

It is about:

- physical presence;
- mental presence;
- emotional presence;
- presence across time;
- intergenerational presence.

A strong internal articulation is:

> **A system for turning love and intention into shared presence across time.**

Traditional consumer products optimize:

- Daily Active Users;

- retention;
- notification opens;

Anuvritti should optimize something different.

## Primary North Star

### **Meaningful Moments Created**

A Meaningful Moment occurs when Anuvritti helps convert an intention into a real family interaction.

Examples:

- saved experiment → performed together;
- saved recipe → cooked together;
- saved toy → thoughtfully considered at the right age;
- saved movie → watched together;
- saved joke → told at breakfast;

- future letter → opened when appropriate;
- grandfather story → listened to;
- saved place → visited;

Anuvritti succeeds when something **leaves the phone and enters life**.

# 7. The Four Core Verbs

The product can be understood through four human actions.

## 7.1 NOTICE

Something matters.

A reel.

A joke.

A phrase.

A toy.

A thought.

A voice.

A place.

A story.

A song.

A memory.

Capture it quickly.

---

## 7.2 REMEMBER

Preserve enough context to keep the meaning.

- Who is this for?
- What is it?
- Why did it matter?
- What did I hope to do?
- When might it become relevant?

AI should do most of the organizational work.

---

## 7.3 RETURN

Bring meaningful things back when they are relevant.

Not as notification spam.

Not as guilt.

Not because “engagement dropped.”

As an opportunity.

---

## 7.4 LIVE

Turn an intention into something real.

- watch;
- cook;
- build;
- buy;
- teach;
- visit;
- laugh;
- talk;
- create;
- call;
- listen;
- simply be together.

Then preserve a tiny trace if desired.

Life continues.

# 8. The Sacred Product Principles

## 8.1 Human Before AI

AI assists the relationship.

It never becomes the relationship.

Anuvritti should often say:

> “Ask Papa.”

rather than pretending to replace Papa.

---

## 8.2 Capture Must Take Seconds

Parents will not maintain elaborate databases.

The ideal interaction is:

**Share → Anuvritti → Done**

Voice should often be easier than typing.

---

## 8.3 Resurfacing Matters More Than Saving

Saving is easy.

Remembering at the right time is valuable.

---

## 8.4 Child Screen Time Is a Cost

The product should frequently encourage the family to leave the app.

A successful flow can end with:

> “Phone goes away now.”

---

## 8.5 No Guilt

Anuvritti must never manufacture parental guilt.

No:

- missed-parenting streaks;
- “you haven't called enough” warnings;
- comparison scores;
- emotionally manipulative notifications.

Every suggestion must be safely skippable.

Sometimes:

> “Nothing needs doing today.”

is the right answer.

---

## 8.6 Memory Is Sacred

Private family data must never become advertising inventory.

No behavioural ads.

No sale of child profiles.

No public-model training on family data by default.

No hidden commercialization of memories.

---

## 8.7 AI Is Not Historical Truth

The system must clearly distinguish:

### Recorded Truth
Actual photos, audio, video, text, and source material.

### Human Interpretation
What Dad, Mum, child, or family member wrote or said.

### AI Interpretation
Machine-generated classification, inference, summaries, age estimates, or suggested meaning.

AI inference must never silently become family history.

---

## 8.8 Not Everything Needs to Be Remembered

The product must leave room for:

- silence;
- forgetting;
- privacy;
- moving on;
- imperfect memories;
- things a child later chooses to hide.

Anuvritti should preserve life, not turn life into a museum.

# 11. Universal Capture

Universal Capture is the first critical interaction.

The user should be able to capture from:

- share sheet;
- browser;
- screenshot;
- camera;
- voice;
- text;
- photo library.

Example:

A father sees a reel about a science experiment.

He taps:

**Share → Anuvritti**

The system infers:

- Science activity
- Likely age: 4–6
- Intent: Do together
- Child: selected child
- Approximate effort: low/medium
- Possible future relevance

Then:

> ✨ Saved.

Optional:

> **What made you save this?** 🎙️

The father says:

> “This looks ridiculous. I want to see his face when it launches.”

Done.

Target capture time:

**under 10 seconds**

# 12. The “Why” Layer ❤️

This may become the most valuable human metadata in the system.

The product should occasionally ask:

> **Why did this make you think of him?**

Voice is preferred.

The answer may be only five seconds.

But compare:

**Toy → Age 3**

with:

> “I never had something like this growing up and would've loved it.”

The second survives decades.

The product should preserve meaning, not just metadata.

---

# 13. Intent Engine

The core AI capability is not generic content classification.

It is **intent understanding**.

The system must distinguish:

- “This is interesting.”
- “I want to buy this.”
- “I want to do this with him.”
- “I want him to watch this someday.”
- “I want to teach him this.”
- “This reminds me of my childhood.”
- “I want to remember this.”
- “This may be useful later.”

Illustrative inferred structure:

```text
object: balance bike
category: toy/activity
likely_intent: consider buying
age_range: 2–5
developmental_relevance: balance / motor activity
relationship: parent → child
urgency: future
confidence: medium
```

Every AI-derived field should conceptually support:

```text
value
source
confidence
human_override
```

# 14. The Return Engine

Saving is not the core value.

Returning is.

The system continuously asks:

> **What previously captured intention may have become relevant now?**

Future signals may include:

- child age;
- developmental stage;
- weekend;
- weather;
- location;
- school calendar;
- upcoming travel;
- birthday;
- family availability;
- recent interests;
- previous activities;
- season;
- budget;
- explicit family goals.

Example:

> ✨ **Worth Bringing Back**
>
> You saved this 8 months ago.
>
> You thought he might enjoy it once he was a little older.
>
> He may be ready now.
>
> **Maybe later**
> **Let's do it**
> **Not relevant anymore**

No guilt.

No fake urgency.

# 24. Preserve Imperfection

Anuvritti should not create a polished highlight reel.

Real family memories include:

- burnt pancakes;
- failed science experiments;
- crying during bicycle practice;
- bad drawings;
- rain ruining the picnic;
- Dad forgetting something;
- embarrassing jokes;
- child's savage feedback.

Often these become the memories people treasure.

But every memory must support:

- private;
- family-only;
- parent-only;
- child-visible;
- hidden later by child.

Failure must never become humiliation.

The system can organize saved toys by:

- child;
- age;
- developmental reason;
- play pattern;
- price range;
- duplicate concept;
- status.

Example:

> You've saved 11 balance bikes over 8 months.
>
> Your child is approaching the age you saved them for.
>
> Here are the 3 strongest candidates.
>
> You may not need to buy one yet.

# 38. Ask My Family — Long-Term Research Concept

Much later, accumulated family memories could support questions like:

> “What did Papa think about failure?”

But the answer must come from real family material.

Example:

> “Your father spoke about failure in 7 recordings.”

Then play the original source.

AI becomes:

> **the librarian of family wisdom, not the author of it.**

This concept requires years of data, careful consent, emotional safety, speaker attribution, and strong governance.

It is not an MVP feature.

---

# 39. Voice Cloning — Research Only

Private voice cloning may one day allow a family member to hear a book read in a parent's synthetic voice.

This is technically possible but ethically dangerous.

If ever explored, requirements include:

- explicit opt-in;
- clear AI-generated label;
- revocability;
- no impersonation;
- family-only use;
- strong consent;
- no synthetic “new advice” presented as historical truth.

This remains research, not roadmap commitment.

# 42. Suggested Core Data Model

# 43. Link-Rot & Copyright Principle

Anuvritti should not assume it can permanently download and store third-party social media content.

For external media, the product should generally preserve:

- original URL;
- creator/source name where permitted;
- title/description where permitted;
- permitted preview data;
- date saved;
- parent voice/text note;
- inferred category;
- intended child;
- intent;
- relevant age/context.

If the external link disappears, the Spark must still retain meaning.

A Spark should never become empty just because the internet changed.

# 44. Privacy Architecture Is Product Architecture

Family data may include:

- child photos;
- child voice;
- family voice;
- relationships;
- locations;
- habits;
- private stories;
- future letters;
- family history;
- emotional context.

Therefore privacy cannot be postponed.

Core principles:

- encryption in transit;
- encryption at rest;
- strong family authentication;
- fine-grained visibility;
- secure media storage;
- export everything;
- delete everything;
- no public-model training by default;
- clear AI provenance;
- controlled sharing;
- age-sensitive permissions;
- auditable changes where appropriate;
- local/on-device processing where useful and feasible.

Potential future target:

**local-first or privacy-first architecture with encrypted sync**

This requires dedicated architecture research.

# 45. Child Data Rights

A two-year-old cannot meaningfully consent.

A fourteen-year-old may care deeply.

The platform should eventually support a progressive rights model.

Possible future stages:

### Young child
Parent-managed.

### Growing child
Shared participation.

### Teenager
Increasing privacy and control.

### Adult
Meaningful ownership over their story.

Potential child rights:

- access;
- hide;
- delete;
- make private;
- decide who can view;
- seal memories;
- export personal content.

A parent capturing a memory **about** a child should not automatically imply permanent parental ownership.
